Facilities & Campus

A campus built for learning, sport and growth

One of the oldest schools in the country, set on a sprawling green campus with modern labs, a well-stocked library and space for every kind of activity.

Learning Spaces

Modern facilities, renewed for today's learners

Science Laboratories

Well-equipped Physics, Chemistry and Biology labs — including a newly renovated Science Lab — give students hands-on scientific learning.

Thiru Vi Ka Library

A rich collection of books, periodicals, newspapers and magazines, with a TV, DVD player and broadband internet — inaugurated in its new form in 2021.

Computer Centre

Established in 1989 and modernised with 30 desktop computers, offering graded computer education to all students from Std. VI to XII.

The Grounds

Space to play, compete and grow

  • A large campus in the heart of Chennai, filled with trees and open space.
  • A big playground for Football, Hockey and Cricket.
  • Courts for Basketball, Volleyball, Badminton and Tennis.
  • Facilities for conducting athletic events, plus a Smart Classroom.

Beyond the Classroom

Co-curricular life at Wesley

From uniformed services to music and clubs, students find countless ways to lead, serve and express themselves.

NCC Air Wing

A very active NCC Air Wing enrols around one hundred students each year, training cadets in drill, aero modelling and range firing, with annual training and trekking camps.

National Service Scheme (NSS)

Open to Class XI and XII students, with 25 willing students enrolled each year for regular service activities and periodical camps.

Junior Red Cross

Students from Std. VI to XII can join the Junior Red Cross Group, carrying out social-service activities throughout the year.

Scouting

The Scout Group provides regular training, with camping and hiking arranged for members every year.

Thiru Vi Ka Literary Association

Encourages students to develop literary and language skills and to build a healthy spirit of competition.

Choir, Band & Clubs

A school Choir and a 25-instrument Band (with free coaching for gifted children), along with the Eco Club, National Green Corps, Readers Club and Heritage Club.

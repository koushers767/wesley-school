About the School

Two centuries of learning, service and character

Wesley Higher Secondary School has served Chennai since 1818 — one of the oldest schools in the city and a proud part of its educational heritage.

Overview

One of the oldest schools in Chennai

Wesley Higher Secondary School, founded in 1818, has crossed the proud milestone of over 200 years of dedicated educational service. Located in the heart of Chennai at Royapettah, it offers courses from Std. VI to XII in both Tamil and English medium.

The school is managed by the Meston Education and Development Association (MEDA), a Christian ecumenical organisation registered under the Companies Act, with its registered office at Westcott Road, Royapettah, Chennai 600 014.

Our Vision

To promote the overall development of students, irrespective of socio-economic conditions, empowering them to face the challenges of the future through dedicated staff who radiate God's love.

Our Mission

To educate students in a spiritual, scientific and psychological atmosphere so that they become enlightened individuals — with individual attention in co-curricular and extra-curricular activities, and character building through devoted staff.

Our History

A journey that began in 1818

Founded by Rev. James Lynch, an enthusiastic follower of John Wesley, the school grew from a small chapel-school into a landmark institution of Chennai.

  1. 1818

    Rev. James Lynch establishes a small school and chapel at Royapettah, Madras — the founding of the Methodist Mission in India.

  2. 1851

    The school reaches High School status through the efforts of Rev. Dr. Ebenezer Jenkins.

  3. 1937

    Meston College of Education emerges; the Wesley High School serves as its practicing school.

  4. 1978

    Upgraded as a Higher Secondary School for boys, with academic courses at the +2 level.

  5. 1990

    Computer subjects introduced, moving with modern educational trends.

  6. 2018

    Bicentenary celebrations — 200 proud years of dedicated educational service.

Identity

Crest, Motto & Prayer

"In Gloriam Dei Optimi Maximi"

To the glory of God

The School Crest

The shield bears a Cross, the symbol of Christians. Scallop shells signify pilgrims seeking knowledge together; the Lamp represents the spread of knowledge, and the Book represents the Bible. The school colours are Red and Golden Yellow.

The Motto

Inscribed on the crest, the motto expresses the school's guiding principle — that all activities be conducted to the glory of God. A School Anthem was released during the bicentenary celebrations of 2018.

The School Prayer

"Almighty and Everlasting Father… we thank you for bringing us into the fellowship of this school. Be our teacher and guide, for in you is all knowledge and wisdom. Help us to find the talents you have bestowed on us and to use them in the way you desire, that we may glorify your Holy Name." — Amen.

The MEDA Family

Our sister institutions

Wesley is one of several institutions of the Meston Education and Development Association, together serving learners across Chennai.

Meston College of Education

Est. 1937

A teacher-education college affiliated to the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University and re-accredited by NAAC, offering B.Ed., M.Ed., M.Phil. and Ph.D. programmes. It grew out of Wesley College, a pioneering arts college of Chennai (1861–1937).

Emma Foulger Matric. Hr. Sec. School

Est. 1981

A co-educational English-medium school with a pre-primary section and classes from 1 to 12, located at Royapettah, Chennai.

Meston Educational Rehabilitation & Research Centre

Est. 1989

A residential special school for mentally challenged children from the most under-privileged backgrounds, functioning from its campus at Karikattukuppam, Muttukadu, near Chennai.

Parents & School

Parent Teachers Association

“No teacher can substitute the parents; no school can be an alternative for the home.” Parents play a vital role in every child's education.

The school has an active Parent Teachers Association, affiliated to the State PTA and duly registered as per Government Orders. It acts as a link between students, teachers and parents — fostering a cordial relationship, providing facilities and amenities, and creating a conducive atmosphere that improves the learning environment.

An Executive Committee, with the Headmaster as Ex-Officio Secretary and the school Correspondent as Ex-Officio Treasurer, meets every month to review improvements. Special counselling sessions are also held for the parents of Class IX and XI students.

Welcome to Wesley

A campus in the heart of Chennai, rich in history and green space

Wesley School is one of the oldest schools in Chennai. What began as a small school and chapel at Royapettah in 1818 grew into a High School by 1851 and a Higher Secondary School for boys, offering courses from Std. VI to XII under the State Syllabus of the Government of Tamil Nadu.

Set on a sprawling, tree-filled campus with a large playground, the school is one of the institutions of the Meston Education and Development Association (MEDA), a Christian ecumenical organisation.

A Sporting Legacy

From our grounds to the Olympic podium

Wesley's football and hockey teams have won titles at district and state level for generations — and two Wesleyans have stood on the Olympic podium for India.

Olympic Gold — Moscow 1980

Sri V. Baskaran

Captain of the Indian Hockey Team that won gold at the 1980 Moscow Olympics; also part of the 1976 Montreal squad. Recipient of the Arjuna Award (1979) and Padma Shri (1980).

Two-time Olympic Bronze

Sri Krishnamurthy Perumal

Member of the Indian Hockey Team that won bronze at the 1968 Mexico and 1972 Munich Olympics. Recipient of the Arjuna Award (1971).

Alumni

Once a Wesleyan, always a Wesleyan

Wesleyans are spread across the globe. The Wesley Alumni Association keeps that community connected and gives back to the school that shaped them.

Prominent Alumni

Wesleyans who shaped the nation

From the Supreme Court to Olympic gold, from literature to public service — Wesley's alumni have left their mark across every field.

Hon'ble Justice Doraiswamy Raju

Former Judge of the Supreme Court of India (2000–2004).

Sri V. Baskaran

Captain of the Indian Hockey Team that won the Olympic Gold Medal, 1980.

Sri M. C. Rajah (1883–1943)

Renowned national leader who worked for the upliftment of the socially depressed classes.

Prof. N. Sivaraj (1892–1964)

Former Mayor of the Corporation of Madras and former Member of Parliament.

Sri V. T. Titus (1919–2006)

Eminent educationist; Director of Collegiate Education and Vice Chancellor of Annamalai University.

Sri Parithi Ilamvazhuthi (1959–2018)

Sri Balakumaran (1946–2018)

Renowned Tamil writer and novelist.

Sri K. N. Venkataramanan, IAS (Retd.)

Distinguished civil servant.

Sri Vinu Chakravarthi & Sri Chinni Jayanth

Popular film actors of Tamil cinema.

Rev. John Rathinam

A pioneer who strived for the rights of people belonging to the depressed classes.

Wesley Alumni Association

A registered charitable trust

The Wesley Alumni Association (WAA) is registered under the Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act, 1975, and under Section 12A of the Income Tax Act, 1961, as a charitable trust.

WAA invites all Wesleyans to become members and to contribute generously towards infrastructure development projects for the school. Life Membership is ₹1,000. Contributions are eligible for deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act.

Giving Back

What the Alumni Association has built

Over the years, Wesleyans have funded and delivered a remarkable range of projects that continue to benefit today's students.

New Computer Lab

A 1,000 sq.ft. computer lab with 30 desktop computers, sponsored by the WAA, was inaugurated on 15th August 2021.

Students' Toilet Complex

Renovation sponsored by the WAA with generous support of ₹5 lakhs from Samatvam Trust.

Smart & Virtual Classrooms

A smart classroom with a video projector for video-conferencing, with virtual classroom systems sponsored by 1979-batch alumni.

Free Grocery & Ration Kits

Kits distributed to parents of 150 poor students during the pandemic, sponsored by alumni, teachers and staff.

Free Medical & Health Camp

Organised in Feb 2019 by 1974–75 batch alumni; over 550 students and 320 parents were screened.

Assembly Hall & Rev. James Lynch Hall

Assisted in renovating the beloved Assembly Hall and constructing the Noon Meal Hall for students.

RO Drinking Water Plant

A reverse-osmosis plant installed through the Lions Club, giving students quality drinking water.

Concrete Benches by the Playground

Ten concrete benches built around the playground for students to sit, watch games and have lunch under the tree shade.

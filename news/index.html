News & Events

Celebrating our students' achievements

Sporting triumphs, academic honours and campus milestones — a snapshot of recent life at Wesley.

July 2025

Runners-Up, Dhaanish Champion League

Wesley School team secured the Runner-Up position in the Dhaanish Champion League Football Tournament.

February 2025

State-Level Football Champions

Wesley School team won the Swami Vivekananda State-level Football Tournament held at Yelagiri.

11 November 2024

First Among Chennai District Schools — SIDP Award

Wesley secured the First Position among Chennai District Schools in the School Innovation Development Project of the Government of Tamil Nadu, receiving a cash prize of ₹1,00,000.

September 2023

First Prize in Speech Competition

Our student Tamil Selvan won First Prize in an inter-school speech competition — a proud reflection of the school's focus on confidence and communication.

2023

Public Speaking Training Programme

A training programme on public speaking was conducted to build confidence and communication skills among our students.

2023

Students Felicitated for Rocket Science Seminar

Dr A. Sivathanu Pillai distributed certificates to Wesley students who participated in the Rocket Science Seminar at Anna University, Guindy, Chennai.

April 2023

Renovated Science Lab Inaugurated

A newly renovated Chemistry / Science Laboratory was inaugurated to give students a modern, hands-on learning environment.

April 2023

Meritorious Sports Students Felicitated

Students who excelled in sports were felicitated for their achievements, celebrating Wesley's proud sporting tradition on and off the field.

15 August

Independence Day Celebrated

The school community came together to celebrate India's Independence Day with the national flag, patriotic songs and an address by the chief guest.

15 August 2021

New Computer Centre with 30 Systems

A new Computer Centre with 30 desktop computers was inaugurated, extending computer education to all students from Std. VI to XII.

15 August 2021

New Thiru Vi Ka Library Inaugurated

The new Thiru Vi Ka Library — with a rich collection of books, periodicals and broadband connectivity — was inaugurated.

May 2020

Free Grocery Kits Distributed as Covid Relief

Free grocery kits — sponsored by Wesley alumni, teachers and staff — were distributed to the parents of 150 poor students, with more kits following in 2021.

December 2019

Reliance State-Level Football Champions

Wesley lifted the Reliance State-Level Football Tournament trophy — one of many titles in the school's proud footballing tradition.

June 2019

Assembly Hall Renovated for the Bicentenary

The school's beloved Assembly Hall — dear to generations of alumni — was renovated as part of the Bicentenary Year, complete with new granite flooring, waterproofing and stained-glass artwork.

9 February 2019

Free Medical & Health Camp

A free medical and health camp organised by Dr Chandrasekhar and the 1974–75 batch alumni screened more than 550 students and 320 parents with a full battery of tests.

8 September 2018

Alumni Reunion & Bicentenary Curtain Raiser

The Alumni Reunion Day and the curtain raiser for the Bicentenary Celebrations were held, with the release of the School Anthem and the Bicentenary logo.

September 2018

The Wesley School Anthem

The School Anthem, "Wesley Nee Vaazhiyave!", was composed and released during the bicentenary celebrations — a proud tribute to 200 years of Wesley. Tap to read the full lyrics.

2017

Bi-Centenary Rev. James Lynch Block Inaugurated

A new classroom block, named after founder Rev. James Lynch, was inaugurated as part of the school's bicentenary development.

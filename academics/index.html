Academics

Courses that build strong foundations and open futures

Wesley offers a full academic pathway from Std. VI to XII, following the syllabus of the Directorate of School Education, Government of Tamil Nadu.

Academic Pathway

From middle school to Higher Secondary

Middle & Secondary (Std. VI–X)

Following the S.S.L.C. syllabus of the Directorate of School Education, Government of Tamil Nadu, with the medium of instruction in Tamil and English.

Higher Secondary (Std. XI–XII)

A two-year course at the +2 level with the medium of instruction in Tamil and English, offering a choice of academic groups.

Assessment

Tests & Examinations

Periodical tests and examinations are conducted for all classes throughout the year:

  • Monthly Tests are conducted in June, August and October.
  • Mid-Term Tests are conducted in July, November and February.
  • Quarterly Examination (First Summative) is held in September.
  • Half-Yearly Examination (Second Summative) is held in December.
  • Annual Examination (Third Summative) is held in March / April.
  • Std. X (SSLC) and Std. XII students sit three Revision Examinations (January–February) in preparation for the public examinations.

Discipline

Rules & Regulations

Uniform

The school uniform consists of navy-blue trousers, half-sleeved white shirts with the school's badge stitched on the pocket, a belt, black shoes and black socks. Shirts are to be tucked in.

  • Students shall attend class regularly and punctually.
  • Students are expected to keep classrooms and premises clean and use the litter bins.
  • Care must be taken of all school property; any damage must be paid for.
  • Students should at all times show respect and deference to the school authorities.
  • The school expects every student to conduct themselves as God-fearing young men — useful to themselves, their parents and their country.
